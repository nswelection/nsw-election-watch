Kiama & South Coast

Labor promises $6.8b South Coast rail fix days after timetable backflip

NSW Government · ABC News · Sydney Morning Herald · The Daily Telegraph · Illawarra Mercury · Milton Ulladulla Times

The NSW government says a re-elected Minns Labor government would commit $6.8 billion to a South Coast Rail Plan, electrifying the Kiama to Bomaderry service and doubling tracks at key bottlenecks, describing it as the largest single heavy rail investment for the Illawarra and South Coast in generations. The ABC reports the promise comes days after proposed timetable changes were abandoned, while the Sydney Morning Herald notes the pledge was made less than five months out from the state election. The Illawarra Mercury reports commuters reacting that the upgrade "should have happened years ago".
