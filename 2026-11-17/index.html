Government & Policy

Minns shelves underquoting laws amid housing slump and backlash from Sydney agents

Sydney Morning Herald · ABC News · The Guardian

The Sydney Morning Herald reports the Minns government has shelved laws that would have placed new restrictions on real estate agents to give buyers more price transparency, citing a housing slump and opposition from Sydney agents. The ABC reports the shelved changes include the requirement for agents to advertise a selling price or price range, with the halt coming a day before the laws were to take effect. The Guardian notes the move is one of several backdowns announced by Premier Chris Minns in recent weeks as the state heads to a March election.
