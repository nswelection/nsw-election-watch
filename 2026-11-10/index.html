Election & Campaign

‘Outrageous and racist’: premier responds to NSW One Nation candidate’s claim Australians are being ‘demographically replaced’

The Guardian · Sydney Morning Herald · Australian Financial Review

Premier Chris Minns and the race discrimination commissioner have criticised comments by Stuart Bonds, who leads One Nation's NSW upper house ticket, after he said at a protest that "we are being demographically replaced", according to reports by the Guardian, the Sydney Morning Herald and the Australian Financial Review. The Premier described the remarks as "racist" and "outrageous", and the AFR reports Bonds has been accused of importing a theory associated with US politics.
