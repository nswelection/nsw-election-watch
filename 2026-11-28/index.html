Today's top lineNSW's budget outlook dominated the day, with Treasurer Daniel Mookhey flagging a further write-down of stamp duty and land tax revenue that the Sydney Morning Herald reports could reach $16 billion, and the AFR reporting the revenue hit will cost the state its surplus next year. Labor also put a $6.8 billion South Coast rail package on the table for the March election, while the Auditor-General reported "significant deficiencies" in Powerhouse Museum contracts. Government & Policy

Stamp duty freefall could cost NSW budget $16 billion

SMH · AFR

Treasurer Daniel Mookhey says a volatile property market will force a further write-down of stamp duty and land tax revenue, which the Sydney Morning Herald reports could cost the budget $16 billion. The AFR reports falling house prices have wiped about 3 per cent off state revenue and will cost NSW its surplus next year, with the treasurer attributing the hit to interest rate rises and tax changes. A separate Herald report puts $8 billion of lost revenue down in part to changes made in the federal budget.
