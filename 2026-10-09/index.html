Government & Policy

Chris Minns says activists shouldn’t get ‘overexcited’ about ‘technical’ landmark coalmine decision

The Guardian · AFR · The Daily Telegraph · The Australian · South Coast Register

The High Court has ruled that a 22-year extension to the Mount Pleasant coal mine was invalid, and the AFR reports Premier Chris Minns has rejected industry calls to change planning laws in response. The Guardian reports Minns described the judgment as technical and said it would not affect state regulation, while the AFR reports miners are bracing for further environmental litigation. The Australian reports Angus Taylor has urged Prime Minister Anthony Albanese to intervene over the ruling, and the South Coast Register carried a wire report in which a premier is said to have pledged further action to tighten state laws protecting fossil fuels.
