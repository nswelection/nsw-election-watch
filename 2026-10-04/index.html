'Historic' result for Greens despite byelection loss as major parties slide

The Newcastle Herald reports that independent Gavin Morris won the Newcastle byelection in a landslide with almost 50 per cent of the vote, while the Greens' Charlotte McCabe finished second in what the paper describes as a historic result for the party.
