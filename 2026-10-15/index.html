Court overturns Tanya Plibersek’s protection order which stalled controversial NSW goldmine

The Guardian · ABC News · SMH

The Federal Court has overturned the Aboriginal heritage protection order issued in 2024 by then federal environment minister Tanya Plibersek over the McPhillamys goldmine at Blayney in central-west NSW, which the company said had effectively blocked the project. The ABC reports the court found the Commonwealth decision was made without authority, after a challenge by Regis Resources.
