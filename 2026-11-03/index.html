Government & Policy

National Gun Buyback in NSW begins today, industry support grants open

NSW Government · ABC News · News.com.au · 7NEWS · The Australian · Milton Ulladulla Times

The Minns government says the biggest gun buyback in 30 years started in NSW on Monday, with the first firearms surrendered and industry support grants opening; the government frames it as a commitment to reduce the number of guns in the community. News.com.au reports more than 2000 firearms were due to be handed in on day one of the scheme, announced after the Bondi terror attack, and that critics have described it as a "PR exercise". The ABC reports only NSW and the ACT have so far joined the national scheme and that bookings at the six participating police stations are full, while 7NEWS reports hundreds of gun owners have signed up and The Australian reports the Premier has said he is willing to take a political hit over the buyback.
