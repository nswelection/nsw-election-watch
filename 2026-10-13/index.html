Today's top lineNSW politics was dominated on Monday by a fight over election costings, with the Minns government saying it will move this week to amend legislation giving One Nation access to the Parliamentary Budget Office before the March 2027 poll — an offer the party says it will not take up. The government also unveiled an integrity package expanding the NSW Electoral Commission's oversight of lobbyists, drawing commentary that the reforms were watered down, while The Guardian reported documents showing the peak oil and gas body was the only meeting held with a minister's office before the state reopened gas exploration. Election & Campaign

One Nation shuns formal costings process before NSW election

The Australian · SMH · news.com.au · South Coast Register · NSW Government

The NSW government says it will seek this week to amend legislation so One Nation can access the independent Parliamentary Budget Office ahead of the March 2027 election. One Nation has said it will not submit its policies for budget costing, telling the Sydney Morning Herald "We've got our own people", while the Minns government accused the party of operating in a "fantasy land" on costings. Premier Chris Minns framed independent costing as something that "keeps us honest", according to news.com.au.
