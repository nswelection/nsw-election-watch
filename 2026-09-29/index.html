ABC News · Guardian Australia · Sydney Morning Herald

The ICAC has heard that developer Toplace paid a private investigator $134,000 to surveil a former Strathfield mayor, Matthew Blackmore, with the operation described to the inquiry as a "personal matter" for company lawyer and Labor councillor Sharangan Maheswaran. Mr Maheswaran, who is under investigation over allegations of blackmail and illegal surveillance, told the inquiry on Tuesday that he hired the investigator, and the commission heard he allegedly made jokes about spy equipment and the song Every Breath You Take. No findings have been made.
