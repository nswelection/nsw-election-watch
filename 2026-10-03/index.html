Bruce Baird, former federal Liberal MP and NSW minister, dies aged 84

The Guardian · Sydney Morning Herald · ABC News · The Daily Telegraph · South Coast Register

Guardian Australia reports he helped win the Olympics for Sydney and was the father of former premier Mike Baird, ABC journalist Julia Baird and marketing executive Steve Baird. The Daily Telegraph reports the Prime Minister was among those paying tribute, and the South Coast Register reports former prime minister Scott Morrison also led tributes.
