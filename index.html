Most NSW homes destroyed in black summer bushfires were within 100 metres of vegetation

The Guardian

The Guardian reports exclusively on research finding that most NSW homes destroyed in the Black Summer bushfires were within 100 metres of vegetation. According to the report, the findings confirm official advice about high-risk properties and warn that worsening conditions will leave more homes unsafe.
