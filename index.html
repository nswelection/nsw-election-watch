Kiama & South Coast

Overhaul means more South Coast rail services but longer trips

ABC News · Illawarra Mercury · South Coast Register

The ABC reports South Coast passengers will receive more than 200 additional weekly services from 15 November, but with longer journeys to Sydney and changes to airport connections. The Illawarra Mercury reports trips to Sydney Airport will take between 15 and 30 minutes longer from that date, and that commutes to Sydney will lengthen rather than shorten under the new timetable. The South Coast Register reports services will increase but that cuts at key stations have prompted commuter backlash, with delays hitting afternoon services on the day the changes were announced.
