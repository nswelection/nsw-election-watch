Government & Policy

Federal MP loses leg, fighting for life after attack

South Coast Register · Crikey

A 43-year-old man has been charged with multiple offences over what is alleged to have been a targeted, life-threatening attack on independent Indi MP Helen Haines outside her Wangaratta electorate office, according to reports in the South Coast Register and Crikey. Haines is reported to have lost a leg and to remain in a critical condition after emergency surgery, with colleagues across the parliament expressing support. Crikey reports Prime Minister Anthony Albanese said the incident appeared to be "a deliberate act", and the South Coast Register reports moves are under way to strengthen security for MPs.
