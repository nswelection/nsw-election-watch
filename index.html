Government & Policy

Largest coal mining proposal in state’s history wins approval

SMH · The Guardian · AFR · ABC News

The NSW Independent Planning Commission has approved the Hunter Valley Operations Continuation Project, allowing mining at the Yancoal and Glencore operation to continue until 2045. The Guardian reports the commission found climate change impacts would be "outweighed" by economic benefits, while the AFR puts the project's potential value at about $3 billion. Environmental and climate groups have condemned the decision, with the Guardian reporting it was described as an "infamous betrayal" and the project as a "carbon bomb".
