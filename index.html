Government & Policy

Hundreds take to water for Newcastle coal port protest despite attempt by NSW police to stop them

The Guardian · ABC News

The Guardian reports about 600 Rising Tide activists paddled kayaks and watercraft into the Port of Newcastle on Saturday, with hundreds more onshore, after police and the NSW government sought to prevent the protest. Protesters unfurled a sign reading "coal exports = climate chaos" and called for funding to help coal workers move into new jobs. The ABC reports the group said more than 900 people had registered to take part and that no arrests were made.
