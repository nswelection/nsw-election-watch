Today's top lineWith the 2027 state election drawing closer, the day's running theme was nerves inside NSW Labor: the Sydney Morning Herald reports some of the party's advertising is leaning heavily on Premier Chris Minns as polling slides, while the Australian Financial Review reports Labor MPs are hearing voter anger about federal issues on the doorstep. Minns also cut a press conference short after being heckled by Greens candidate Shane Bazzi, an incident the Greens have rebuked. On policy, the government announced a modern slavery taskforce and a rollout of free ADHD assessments, while the Tenants' Union pressed for rental bond interest to fund the state's rental taskforce.
