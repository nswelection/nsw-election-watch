Government & Policy

Fred Nile, former NSW politician and figurehead of the rightwing Christian Democratic party, dies aged 92

The Guardian · ABC News · Sydney Morning Herald

Former NSW MP and Christian Democratic Party figurehead Reverend Fred Nile has died aged 92, his family says. He sat in the NSW Legislative Council for more than 40 years after first entering the upper house in 1981, retiring after unsuccessfully contesting the 2023 election. Outlets note he was a controversial figure who took positions against abortion, drug use and homosexuality, including opposing same-sex marriage, though in later years he supported legislation expunging historical homosexual convictions.
