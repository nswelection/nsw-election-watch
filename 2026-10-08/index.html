Government & Policy

Hunter Valley community group wins landmark high court climate change case over Mount Pleasant coalmine

The Guardian · ABC News · AFR · The Daily Telegraph

The High Court has ruled that the approval of MACH Energy's Mount Pleasant coalmine expansion at Muswellbrook was invalid, with Guardian Australia reporting it is the first High Court case to consider climate change and that advocates say it sets a binding national precedent for fossil fuel projects in NSW. The AFR reports the court found authorities failed to properly consider emissions from coal that would be exported, and that miners warn the ruling puts investment at risk. The ABC reports the decision could redraw how fossil fuel projects are approved, while Guardian Australia also profiles the retired Hunter couple, Wendy Wales and Tony Lonergan, behind the four-year challenge.
