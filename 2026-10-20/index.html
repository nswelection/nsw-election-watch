Government & Policy

NSW government drafts law to allow Upper Hunter mine to increase output

ABC News · The Guardian · SMH · AFR · The Daily Telegraph · NSW Government

Premier Chris Minns says the government will introduce legislation in the next sitting fortnight to allow MACH Energy's Mount Pleasant coal mine in the Hunter to lift production, with the stated aim of protecting 400 jobs through to 2032. The SMH reports the bill would permit an increase of four million tonnes a year, while the Guardian reports the mine's expansion was subject to a recent High Court decision and that critics have called the bill "legislative overreach"; the ABC reports environment leaders describe the move as government overreach. The AFR reports Minns has described the measure as a single-use solution and warned other resources companies not to expect a legislative fix if courts reject their proposals.
