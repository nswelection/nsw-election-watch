Kiama & South Coast

Back track: Government cancels unpopular changes to South Coast rail timetable

South Coast Register · ABC News · Sydney Morning Herald · The Daily Telegraph

The NSW government has cancelled the rail timetable changes that were due to take effect in November, with the South Coast Register reporting that South Coast commuters had been angered by services no longer stopping at Wolli Creek. The ABC reports the scrapped changes covered both Sydney and Illawarra services and were abandoned less than a month before they were to begin. The Sydney Morning Herald describes the reversal as a U-turn following what it reports as near-universal condemnation, while the Daily Telegraph reports Premier Chris Minns scrapped the shake-up after commuter backlash.
