Infrastructure investment to support future housing growth in West Dapto

NSW Government · Illawarra Mercury

The NSW government says it is allocating $1.4 million to Wollongong City Council for the Northcliffe Drive Extension, which it links to accelerating nearly 850 new homes and supporting a further 19,500 homes across the broader West Dapto area. The Illawarra Mercury reports the council will borrow $28 million to buy land for the extension, with the state contribution going towards interest on that loan, and describes the project as enabling the closure of a level crossing the paper calls "dangerous".
